# SWYNEX Internship - Task 3: Visualization and Interpretation

## 🦠 Project Overview
This repository contains the scientific data visualizations and interpretations comparing the structural genomic architecture (specifically GC Content distribution) of 1,000 isolates across the SARS-CoV-2 Delta (B.1.617.2) and Omicron (B.1.1.529) variant lineages.

---

## 🔬 Scientific Interpretation Report

### 📈 What the Visualizations Prove
* **Lineage-Specific Stability:** The mathematical analysis reveals two highly distinct, tightly grouped normal distributions. The Delta variant maintains a slightly higher average GC content equilibrium centered near **37.98%**, while the Omicron variant shows a minor shift down to **37.94%**.
* **Mutational Pressure:** This clear micro-shift proves that the heavily mutated Omicron Spike (S) protein accumulated an increased ratio of Adenine (A) and Thymine (T) nucleotide mutations over time, slightly lowering its overall density of Guanine (G) and Cytosine (C) base pairings.

### 🛑 What the Visualizations Do NOT Prove
* **Functional Impact:** While this distribution mathematically proves that a structural genomic density shift happened during viral evolution, it **does not prove** how these mutations physically change the binding affinity to human ACE2 receptors or the exact biological transmission rate. 
* **Patient Severity:** This baseline architectural data chart cannot be used to predict clinical disease severity or patient hospitalization outcomes without pairing it directly with external patient case histories.
